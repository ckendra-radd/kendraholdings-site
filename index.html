Principles
How we operate
01

Principal‑led

Every acquisition is sourced, negotiated, and operated by the principal. No committees, no intermediaries between you and a decision.

02

Permanent hold

We buy businesses to keep them. No fund cycle, no resale timeline, no intention of selling.

03

Operations first

Value comes from running the business well — modern equipment, honest pricing, and service the neighborhood can rely on.

04

Financing discipline

Conservative structures, transparent terms, and debt the business itself can comfortably carry.

Focus

Essential, self‑serve, close to home.

Our focus is self‑serve essential services — beginning with laundry. These are businesses people depend on every week, in every economy, run best by owners who are physically present in the community they serve.

We concentrate in the Pioneer Valley and greater Hampden County, where we live and work, and where being twenty minutes away is a durable operating advantage.
